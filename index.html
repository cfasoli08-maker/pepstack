Retatrutide
GIP / GLP-1 / Glucagon Triple Agonist
▾
Half-life: ~6 days
Route: SubQ
Storage: Refrigerate
Mechanism
Triple receptor agonist acting on GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. Significantly more potent than dual agonists for metabolic and body composition outcomes.
Primary Uses
Body composition, metabolic health, appetite regulation, insulin sensitivity. One of the most powerful compounds available for fat loss and metabolic optimization.
Synergies
Stacks well with MOTS-c for metabolic enhancement. NAD+ supports cellular energy demands during active recomposition.
Protocol Notes
Titrate slowly. Nausea is common at higher doses early on. Typical titration: 2mg increments every 4-5 weeks. Do not escalate if side effects are present.
MOTS-c
Mitochondrial-Derived Peptide
▾
Half-life: ~3-6 hrs
Route: SubQ or IV
Storage: Freeze lyophilized
Mechanism
Encoded in mitochondrial DNA. Regulates metabolic homeostasis, activates AMPK pathway, and mimics the cellular effects of exercise.
Primary Uses
Metabolic health, insulin sensitivity, exercise performance, anti-aging, weight regulation. Often called a natural exercise mimetic.
Synergies
Powerful with Retatrutide for metabolic optimization. NAD+ complements by supporting the same mitochondrial energy pathways.
Protocol Notes
Best administered 30-60 minutes before physical activity. Effects are cumulative over weeks. 3x per week is the established frequency.
GHK-Cu
Copper Tripeptide
▾
Half-life: ~1-2 hrs
Route: SubQ / Topical
Storage: Refrigerate
Mechanism
Naturally occurring copper complex that stimulates collagen and elastin production, activates antioxidant enzymes, and promotes tissue remodeling.
Primary Uses
Skin regeneration, anti-aging, hair growth, tissue repair, wound healing, anti-inflammatory. Exceptionally safe and well-tolerated for daily use.
Synergies
BPC-157 stacks well for tissue repair. Can be used topically alongside SubQ for localized skin benefits.
Protocol Notes
One of the safest daily-use peptides. Light sensitive; amber vials preferred. Blue-green tint in solution is normal from copper content.
★ Your Favourite
NAD+
Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide
▾
Half-life: Minutes
Route: SubQ / IV
Storage: Freeze lyophilized
Mechanism
Essential coenzyme in every cell. Drives ATP production, activates sirtuins (longevity proteins), supports DNA repair, and regulates circadian rhythm. Declines significantly with age.
Primary Uses
Energy, cognitive clarity and focus, anti-aging, metabolic function, cellular repair. SubQ delivery produces fast and noticeable effects especially for mental energy.
Synergies
Excellent with MOTS-c — both target mitochondrial function. Retatrutide and NAD+ together support comprehensive metabolic optimization.
Protocol Notes
Short cycles prevent downregulation. 3x per week is the effective maintenance frequency. Some experience a mild flush after injection — normal response.
BPC-157
Body Protection Compound
▾
Half-life: ~4 hrs
Route: SubQ / Oral
Storage: Freeze lyophilized
Mechanism
Synthetic pentadecapeptide derived from a protective protein in gastric juice. Promotes angiogenesis, accelerates tissue healing, modulates nitric oxide pathways.
Primary Uses
Injury recovery (tendon, ligament, muscle, bone), gut and GI tract healing, inflammation reduction. One of the most versatile healing compounds available.
Protocols
Injury: 10 units twice daily at or near the injured site. Gut health: 10 units once daily SubQ. Never run both simultaneously.
Synergies
TB-500 is the classic partner for accelerated healing. GHK-Cu complements for tissue repair and collagen regeneration.
